Delete your RideMate account

RideMate · Account and data deletion

Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open RideMate and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the settings (gear) button, then Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and press and hold “Hold to delete my account”.

This immediately and permanently deletes from our servers: your profile and photo, posts, photos, videos, comments and likes, riding buddies, your garage showcase, groups you own (and your memberships), group rides you organised and your RSVPs, live-ride positions and messages, SOS records, vehicles, planned trips, preferences, hazard reports and live-location links. The app then erases everything stored on the phone (rides, contacts, documents, offline maps).

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app, email 1996salman.01khan@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my RideMate account” and your display name, city and, if you can, a post or group of yours so we can identify the account. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What we keep

A minimal audit record that an account deletion happened (account ID and date, no personal details), and database backups that are overwritten within 7 days. Content you posted inside other riders' groups is deleted with your account.
