Privacy Policy

RideMate · Effective 12 October 2026

RideMate is a motorcycle riding companion: navigation, trip planning, ride recording, safety tools and a riders' community. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version

  • No ads, no analytics trackers, no selling or renting of your data — ever.
  • Most of your data (rides, GPS tracks, expenses, medical ID, emergency contacts, document vault) stays only on your phone.
  • Your location is shared with other people only when you choose: a live group ride, SOS, a live-location link, or a hazard report.
  • You can delete your account and all server data from inside the app at any time.

1. Data that stays on your phone

Stored in the app's private storage on your device and never uploaded by us:

  • Recorded rides and GPS tracks, ride expenses and ratings
  • Medical ID (blood group, medical notes) and emergency contacts (names and phone numbers)
  • Document vault (photos of licence, RC, insurance, PUC and similar) — these never leave the phone
  • Offline maps, saved places (home/work), settings, checklists, bike-care counters

Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Erase all data removes it. Android may include app data in your own Google device backup if you have that enabled.

2. Data stored on our servers

Community and sync features use an account created automatically and anonymously (no email or phone number is required). Our database is hosted by Supabase (data centre in Mumbai, India). We store:

  • Profile: display name, bike, city, bio, riding experience and an optional profile photo
  • Community content: posts, photos, videos, comments, likes, groups you create or join, group rides and RSVPs
  • Riding buddies: buddy requests you send or accept (only you and the other rider can see the request; other riders see only how many buddies you have)
  • Garage showcase: for bikes you choose to show on your profile — make, model, nickname, year, your description, photos and videos. The number plate is never uploaded.
  • Garage and preferences: vehicles (make, model, fuel type, tank, mileage, range — not the registration number), notification and riding preferences
  • Planned trips: route, stops, itinerary days, dates and budget assumptions, so they sync with your account
  • Live group rides: while you are in a live ride, your latest position, speed and status are shared with riders on that ride only, and deleted when you leave; ride messages and SOS alerts you send to the group
  • Hazard reports: the type and location of hazards you report (pothole, police check, accident…), shown to other riders for a limited time
  • Live-location links: if you share one, your position while the link is active; the link stops working when you end it or it expires

3. Services that process requests for us

To work without a server of our own, the app sends limited data directly to these providers. Each receives your IP address and only what it needs:

  • OpenFreeMap — map tiles for the area you view
  • Valhalla (FOSSGIS) — start, stop and destination coordinates to calculate routes
  • Photon (komoot) — what you type in search, with your approximate location to rank results
  • Overpass API (OpenStreetMap) — the map area around you or your route, to find fuel, mechanics, hospitals and stays
  • Open-Meteo — coordinates for weather forecasts
  • Google speech services — when you use voice commands, Android's speech recogniser processes your voice under Google's own privacy policy; text-to-speech runs on the phone
  • Supabase — hosting of the data in section 2

None of these providers receive your name or account details from us.

4. Permissions

  • Location (precise): navigation, nearby places, ride recording, SOS and live rides. While you record a ride or navigate, a visible notification shows that RideMate keeps using location with the screen off. We do not use location in the background otherwise.
  • Microphone: only while voice commands are listening (you start it, or turn on hands-free mode).
  • Notifications: the ride-recording notification, safety alerts and reminders.
  • Camera / photos / videos: only when you pick a photo or video for a post, your profile, your garage or the document vault.
  • SMS and phone calls: used only for SOS — to text your emergency contacts your location and call for help when you press SOS or a crash is detected and you don’t cancel. Texts go from your own SIM. RideMate never reads your messages or call history.
  • Motion sensors: crash detection during a ride, processed on the phone.

5. SOS and emergencies

When you press SOS (or a crash is detected and you don’t cancel the countdown), RideMate texts your emergency contacts directly from your phone with your location and, when available, a live-location link, and can call your emergency number. If you haven’t allowed texts, it opens your messaging app with the message ready to send. During a live group ride, an SOS alert with your location also goes to the riders on that ride. RideMate is not an emergency service; always call 112 in an emergency.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Profile → Settings → Hold to delete my account. This permanently deletes your profile, posts, photos, comments, groups you own, RSVPs, vehicles, trips, preferences and hazard reports from our servers, then erases the app's data on the phone. You can also request deletion on the web: https://salmanasakhan.github.io/ridemate-site/delete-account.html.
  • Stop sharing: leave a live ride or end a live-location link at any time; sharing stops immediately.
  • Access or correct: edit your profile and content in the app, or email us for a copy of your server data.
  • Withdraw permissions in Android settings at any time; features that need them will stop working.

We handle personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Backups of the database that our hosting provider may keep are overwritten within 7 days.

7. Children

RideMate is meant for riders aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Server access is restricted with row-level security so riders can only change their own data, and live-ride positions are visible only to riders on that ride.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
